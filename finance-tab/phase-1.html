Context
The Finance tab turns the books into a read-mostly view in the Northlight OS, one phase at a time. Rules for every phase: a mockup is approved before a screen is built, an AIMS-004 amendment is approved before any new write, and every change is a pull request that Elizabeth merges. The full plan is dashboard/specs/FINANCE-TAB-PROJECT-BRIEF.md in the dispatcher repo.

Decisions
Accounting method is accrual
Multi-month contracts. No accountant at the moment; confirm when one is engaged. (20 Nov)
Keep records 7 years
CRA requires 6 after the tax year; one extra year because items carry from year to year. (20 Nov)
Finance policies live in a separate ClickUp document
Keeps the compliance agent scope unchanged and lets policies change without an amendment. Not created yet. (20 Nov)
Corrections are adjusting entries, never edits
Keeps the original rows for the tax record and the audit trail. (11 Nov)
Books in CAD; foreign amount and rate come from the card statement at reconciliation
A count of foreign-currency transactions is the minimum. (20 Nov)
Old rows without a receipt link are matched read-only, not edited
The OS never edits an existing log row (AIMS-004 A4 4.1). (20 Nov)
Regenerating a report is always Elizabeth's press
